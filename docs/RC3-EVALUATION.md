2026-11-24. Package evaluated: the downloaded folder "Higgsfield Director PRO v4.1 RC3", generated by ChatGPT. The
package **is not in this repository**: it stays in the operator's local copy. Below is what came in it and what we kept
from each part. Everything was measured, and the last column of the tables says how.

## 1. In one sentence

RC3 had a good method skeleton (briefing → shots → continuity bibles → cost → score with evidence) and a good camera
and lighting vocabulary (the one from v1), but the rest was filler, the three compilers failed on common cases, the
knowledge about models was empty and out of date, and nothing talked to the official Higgsfield connector. v5 rewrites
it lean, keeping ~15% of the content.

## 3. Measured defects (all became tests in `higgsfield-director/tests/test_hfd.py`)

**They said the wrong thing:**
1. It removed the audio from Cinema Studio and sent it to "post-production" (it read the matrix where everything is
   `null`); the catalog says Cinema Studio 4.0 generates audio by default.
2. An unknown model (`kling-3.0`) fell back to the "universal" profile with no warning.
3. A shot without `end_state` turned off the boundary check: the next shot could start with the product in another
   color.
4. Outdated model profiles: "sora" (out of the catalog; discontinued according to the press), no Kling 4.0,
   Seedance 2.5, Veo 3.1, Wan 3.0, MiniMax H3 or Gemini Omni.
5. `SKILL.md` cited `references/v2.1-original.zip`, which does not exist.

**They blocked use:**
6. "static camera, no orbit", "locked-off shot, no pan or dolly" and "slow orbit around a static subject" were
   **rejected** (an error, not a warning) by all three compilers.
7. The RC4 MCP took 4 of the 25 fields.
8. `tempo`, `cuts`, `reference_count` accepted and ignored; an empty `LOCKS:` line; the prompt chopped into fragments.

**Dead weight:** 116 "commands" in three registries that only pointed to `.md` files; a catalog of 2,010 "recipes" =
212 terms × modifiers, all `unverified`; 200 "DNAs" = 10 × 5 × 4 untested combinations; 165 schematic images (the
"character sheet" repeated the same figure in all 4 views); REST, Docker, CI, mkdocs, LUT, histogram "similarity",
optional embeddings.

**Did not work in Codex:** no Higgsfield MCP configured, paths relative to the skill folder, a description that was
too broad (it would compete for triggers with the existing video skills), no `agents/openai.yaml`.

## 4. What was kept

The v1 vocabulary (`references/legacy/camera.md`, `look.md`, `motion.md`) → `references/vocabulary.md`; templates and
examples (`workflows.md`), the golden rule (`core/prompting.md`), the intent table (`core/decision.md`), 18 recipes
(`master/MANUAL.md`) → `references/prompt.md`; planning, bibles and budget (`production/`) →
`references/workflow.md`; diagnosis and score (`quality/`) → `references/quality.md`; validation, continuity, score
(`scripts/studio.py`), the 180° rule and export (`scripts/ultimate.py`) → `scripts/hfd.py`, rewritten.

## 5. Official facts used in v5

Gathered on 2026-11-24; the source of each one is in `higgsfield-director/references/prompt.md` and `models.md`.

- Higgsfield guide: video is a "shot list"; describe what you want (not what you do not want); English is more
  predictable; use the last frame as the start of the next shot.
- Rules found in two or more official sources (Higgsfield, Google Veo, Kling, OpenAI Sora, Alibaba Wan, MiniMax): a
  subject/action/scene/camera/light structure; one movement and one action per shot; standard camera vocabulary;
  audio requested in a separate block; dialogue labeled and sized to the duration; parameters outside the text;
  iterate one variable at a time.
- Higgsfield terms (2026-10-23): an image of a real person requires rights and consent; minors are protected; follow
  the policy of each model provider.
- Veo 3.1 Preview shut down by Google on 2026-10-22 (the connector still offers the variant); Sora 2 out of the
  catalog.
