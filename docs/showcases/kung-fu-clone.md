# Kung fu vs robot clone

A 10 s fight between the maintainer's own character and a robot clone of himself, at 1080p with sound, made one step
at a time. Record: [the demo page](../demo/README.md) (2026-11-26), which has the video, the prompt as it was sent and
the sound. Credits are as quoted that day: a record, not a price list.

## The request

As the demo page records it: a 10 s fight between the maintainer's own character and a robot clone of himself.

## What the skill did

Each step was quoted first and paid only after an "ok".

| Step | What happened | Credits |
|---|---|---|
| 1. The plan | the fight in three beats, in words: the robot attacks and he takes a punch; he splits into copies of mist; they close in and his spinning kick sends the robot to the floor | 0 |
| 2. Four stills | four places and looks (Soul 2.0, with his character); the first, a dark warehouse, became the start frame | 0.48 |
| 3. The draft | the whole 10 s shot in Seedance 2.5's draft mode (480p), without sound | 30 |
| 4. The final | the same shot at 1080p, made from the approved draft | 160 |
| 5. The sound | added on the computer: 14 sound effects made with ElevenLabs, each hit on the frame of its contact | — |
| **Total on Higgsfield** | | **190.48** |

The idea of splitting into copies joined the plan after the stills and before the draft, while changing it was
still free.

## What was learned

- The cheap steps came first: 0.48 credits of stills chose the look and the start frame before 30 for the draft and
  160 for the final.
- A final made from a draft repeats the draft: 241 frames in both files, the same poses at the same instants. It also
  kept the draft's silence, although the final asked for sound
  ([own runs, lesson 1](../../lessons-learned/2026-11-own-runs.md#1-a-final-made-from-a-draft-repeats-the-draft)).

## What changed in the skill

`references/models.md`, "Test composition cheaply": the final repeats the draft's motion, so the draft is where the
motion is judged; and when the final needs sound, the draft is made with sound.
