# Lessons waiting for a test

What each source showed and said, and a minimal reproduction. None of these has been run. Before running one, quote
it, get the operator's ok, and give it a card of its own, as in [2026-11-25-timed-beats.md](2026-11-25-timed-beats.md).

In none of these sources does the generation panel appear with the settings. Each rests on what the presenter
says and on a judgment made by watching or listening, so each is a hypothesis.

## The previous clip as a video reference

[Batch 1, lesson 11](../2026-11-higgsfield-channel.md#adopted). Video 04, from
[4:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zYPgz6sOy74&t=243s) to [5:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zYPgz6sOy74&t=308s).

- **Shown:** the finished shots. The video panel and its reference field are not shown in the frames checked.
- **Said:** Seedance, 15 s, the prompt with a keyframe and the previous video
  ([4:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zYPgz6sOy74&t=286s)); a few runs, then the best one picked; every shot
  landed.
- **Unknown:** the model version, whether the previous clip went in as a reference or only as a description, and
  how many runs.
- **Reproduction:** two consecutive shots of a generic character. Same start frame and prompt; only the previous
  clip as a video reference varies (with or without), on a model whose catalog entry accepts video references.
  Three attempts per version. Measure continuity of clothes, hair and light against the previous clip, at the start,
  middle and end of each attempt, by someone who does not know which version is which.

## Traffic in a tighter frame

[Batch 2, lesson 21](../2026-11-higgsfield-channel-2.md#adopted). Video 20,
[20:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GNxmt_4IifA&t=1213s) to [21:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GNxmt_4IifA&t=1275s).

- **Shown:** the presenter only, in the frames checked; no traffic clip and no panel.
- **Said:** six shots on the first try; the traffic lights came out wrong in all of them
  ([20:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GNxmt_4IifA&t=1234s)); drone flights and overtaking go wrong; the tighter
  the frame, the fewer mistakes.
- **Unknown:** the model, the duration, and the clips themselves.
- **Reproduction:** a car on a city street. Only the framing varies: wide, with an intersection and traffic lights,
  or tight, with no intersection. Six attempts per version. Measure with a checklist per clip: lights that change
  wrongly, cars passing through each other, lanes ignored.
